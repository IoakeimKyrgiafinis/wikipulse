How to read these numbers:

- **Recall** is the share of articles linked from a day's portal page that had a spike within 24 hours of that day. Many links are generic (countries, cities, organizations), so recall over *lead* links (the first link of each portal entry, usually its subject) is the more meaningful of the two.
- **Precision against the portal** is the share of spikes whose article is on a portal page within 48 hours. It is only a *lower bound*: much genuine news (sports, TV, deaths of minor figures) never reaches the portal.
- **Precision from labels** is measured on this run's random sample of 50 spikes (seed 2026, as drawn by `wikipulse.eval.label`); 50 of them have a label (labelers: llm: 50; models: ollama:qwen3:4b (50)). News share among news and not-news labels, `unsure` left out. LLM labels are only an *estimate* of the truth; a human label always replaces an LLM label.
- **Time to detection** is the time from the first edit of the burst to the spike's start, over the 10 spikes found on the portal (min 6.9, max 50.2 minutes).
